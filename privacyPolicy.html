11. Permissions We Request and Why

To provide our services, our App requests the following permissions on your Android device. These permissions are essential for the App's functionality and are used strictly for the purposes described below:

  • CAMERA: Allows the App to access your device's camera. This is necessary for video calls and for you to share your video feed with other participants during a call.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Allows the App to access your device's microphone. This is necessary for audio calls and for you to share your voice with other participants during a call.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Allows the App to change audio settings, such as speakerphone or headset mode, during calls for optimal audio experience.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & INTERNET: Allows the App to access network information and connect to the internet. These are fundamental for all online features, including making and receiving calls and messages.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the device from sleeping when the App is active, ensuring calls and notifications function correctly without interruption.
  • VIBRATE: Allows the App to control the vibrator, primarily used for notifications (e.g., incoming call alerts).
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Allows the App to draw over other apps. This is used to display incoming call screens or floating widgets even when the App is in the background, ensuring you don't miss calls.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Allows the App to start automatically when the device finishes booting. This helps ensure that you receive call invitations and notifications reliably.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Allows the App to run services in the foreground, which is necessary for ongoing activities like active calls or persistent notifications, ensuring their uninterrupted operation.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the App to post notifications to your device, crucial for alerting you to incoming calls, messages, and other important updates.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Allows the App to use full-screen intents, which are used to display high-priority notifications, such as incoming call screens, even when the device is locked.
  • ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS & READ_PHONE_STATE: Allows the App to answer incoming phone calls and read the device's phone state. These permissions are used to manage the behavior of in-app calls when you receive a native phone call, ensuring a smooth transition and preventing conflicts. We do not record your phone calls or access your call logs.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Allows the App to interact with the Google Play billing service for in-app purchases. We do not directly collect or store your payment information; all transactions are handled securely by Google Play.
